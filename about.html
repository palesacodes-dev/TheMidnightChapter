Our Story

About The Midnight Chapter

Solving the late night study crisis for university students, software developers, and researchers.

Organisation History

Opened in 2024, The Midnight Chapter was established to fix a common issue: the lack of safe, quiet, and reliable study spaces during late nights. What began as a single reading room has grown into a multi branch study lounge operating round the clock.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide a safe, quiet 24/7 environment combining individual study spaces, curated literature, and fresh coffee.


Vision: To become the premier network of 24/7 study hubs for late night coders, researchers, and students.

On Site Security Measures

To ensure total peace of mind during overnight sessions, all facilities feature keycard door locks, full time overnight security guards, and emergency desk communication lines.
