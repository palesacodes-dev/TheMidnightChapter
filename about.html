About The Midnight Chapter

Solving the late night study crisis for university students, software developers, and researchers.

Organisation History

Opened in 2024, The Midnight Chapter was established to fix a problem facing many students: the complete lack of safe, quiet, and reliable study spaces open during late night and early morning hours. What began as a single reading room has grown into a two branch study lounge operating round the clock.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide a safe, quiet 24/7 environment combining individual study spaces, curated literature, and fresh coffee to support productivity at any hour.

Vision: To become the premier nationwide network of 24/7 study hubs for late night coders, researchers, and students.

On-Site Security Measures

To ensure total peace of mind during overnight study sessions, all facilities feature keycard door locks, full time overnight security guards, and emergency desk communication lines.
